> AppreciateYa is a simple way to send a teammate a thank-you, a virtual coffee, or a small gift. I made it for the everyday wins that are easy to overlook when a team works remotely.

---

- 👉 [Launch AppreciateYa](https://appreciate-ya.vercel.app/)
- 💾 [View Code on GitHub](https://github.com/applescan/appreciate.ya)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 💌 **Send Personalized Kudos** – Celebrate teammates with gratitude messages, coffee, or gift cards.
- 🏆 **Track Top MVPs** – Discover who gives and receives the most appreciation.
- 📊 **See Culture Insights** – Get a visual pulse on your team’s appreciation habits.
- 🤖 **AI Suggestions** – Let AI help you write heartfelt notes with just one click!

---

## ✨ Why This Matters

When work gets busy, a quick thank-you can be the first thing to disappear. This was my attempt to make that small gesture easier.

---

## 🧠 New Feature: AI Kudos Writer

Not sure how to word it? The built-in **AI Kudos Generator** offers a starting point you can make your own.
